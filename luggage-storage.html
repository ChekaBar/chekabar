The Service

Hands free, mind at ease.

Whether you have arrived early, checked out late, or just want a day without a suitcase, you can leave your bags at Cheka and pick them up when it suits you.

Every item is tagged when you drop it off and matched to you at pick-up. Bags stay inside the café in a supervised area — not on the pavement — with our team close by throughout opening hours.
